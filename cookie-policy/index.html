Cookie Policy

Cookie Policy of Giga BulletIN

Last Updated: November 2026

Giga Bulletin is an independent digital magazine and blogzine dedicated to poetry, literature, visual arts, creative writing, cultural commentary, and artistic expression. This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used when visitors access or interact with the Giga Bulletin website. It also explains how visitors can manage their cookie preferences and make choices regarding the use of non-essential cookies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function, remember certain preferences, understand visitor activity, and improve the overall browsing experience.

Cookies may be temporary (session cookies) or remain on your device for a specified period (persistent cookies).

How We Use Cookies

Giga Bulletin may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes, depending on the features and services enabled on the website:

  • Essential Cookies: To support basic website functionality, security, navigation, and accessibility.
  • Functional Cookies: To remember visitor preferences and improve the browsing experience.
  • Analytics Cookies: To understand website traffic, visitor interactions, popular pages, and general website performance, where analytics services are enabled.
  • Third-Party Cookies: To support externally provided features or embedded content, where applicable.

Giga Bulletin does not intend to use cookies to collect the actual creative content of poems, stories, articles, paintings, illustrations, or other submissions.

Third-Party Cookies and Embedded Content

Certain features available on Giga Bulletin may rely on external platforms or services. These may include embedded documents, media, social media content, or other externally hosted resources.

Such third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when visitors interact with their content.

The collection and use of information by these external services are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms. Giga Bulletin does not independently control the cookie practices of external service providers.

Cookie Consent and Visitor Choices

Giga Bulletin respects the privacy and choices of its visitors.

Where required, visitors will be provided with a cookie consent notice before non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies are activated.

Visitors may be offered the following options:

  • Accept All: Accept all applicable cookie categories, including non-essential cookies.
  • Reject Non-Essential: Decline cookies that are not necessary for basic website functionality.
  • Manage Preferences: Review and select individual categories of optional cookies.

Essential cookies may remain active where they are necessary for the operation and security of the website.

Visitors may change or withdraw their cookie consent at any time through the available cookie preference settings.

A visitor's decision to reject optional cookies will not prevent access to the general content of Giga Bulletin, although certain external features may not function as intended.

Managing and Deleting Cookies

Visitors can also manage cookies through their internet browser settings.

Most browsers allow users to view, block, restrict, or delete cookies stored on their devices.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some website features or embedded services.

Personal Data and Privacy

Cookies and similar technologies may sometimes process information that can identify or relate to an individual.

Any personal data processed through such technologies will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection requirements.

Giga Bulletin aims to collect and process only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant website purposes.

For further information about the collection, use, and protection of personal information, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

Updates to This Cookie Policy

Giga Bulletin may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, technologies, external services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any revised policy will be published on this page along with an updated revision date.

Visitors are encouraged to review this page periodically.
