Submissions and Contributions

Giga Bulletin welcomes original poetry, essays, short stories, reviews, paintings and other creative works from writers and artists who wish to share their voices with our readers. Submissions can be sent to our email along with a brief introduction.

Giga Bulletin is an independent literary magazine and blogzine run with a passion for poetry, creativity, and cultural expression. If you enjoy our work and would like to support the continued publication of free literary content, you may also choose to make a voluntary donation.

Your support can be as simple as the cost of a cup of tea, a train ticket, a notebook, or any small amount from your daily routine. Every contribution, regardless of size, helps us maintain the website, publish new content, and provide a platform for emerging and established writers.
