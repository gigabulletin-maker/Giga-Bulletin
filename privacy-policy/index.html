Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy of Giga BulletIN

Last Updated: October 2026

Giga Bulletin is an independent digital magazine and blogzine dedicated to poetry, literature, visual arts, creative writing, cultural commentary, and artistic expression. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when contributors and visitors interact with our platform.

Information We Collect

Giga Bulletin may collect information voluntarily provided by:

  • Poets
  • Writers
  • Authors
  • Artists
  • Illustrators
  • Painters
  • Creative contributors
  • Readers and visitors

The information collected may include names, pen names, email addresses, author biographies, social media links, portfolio information, submitted manuscripts, poems, articles, illustrations, paintings, photographs, and other creative works intended for publication or review.

Purpose of Information Collection

Information submitted to Giga Bulletin may be used to:

  • Review creative submissions.
  • Communicate with contributors.
  • Publish accepted literary and artistic works.
  • Credit authors, poets, artists, and illustrators appropriately.
  • Maintain editorial records.
  • Improve the quality and diversity of published content.

Creative Submissions

Contributors affirm that all submitted material is their original work or that they possess the necessary rights and permissions to submit it.

Submission of a work does not guarantee publication. The editorial team reserves the right to accept, reject, edit, or remove content according to the publication's editorial standards and policies.

Intellectual Property and Copyright

Giga Bulletin strongly supports original writing and artistic integrity.

All poems, stories, articles, essays, paintings, illustrations, artworks, photographs, and other creative materials published on Giga Bulletin remain the intellectual property of their respective creators unless otherwise stated.

No content published on Giga Bulletin may be reproduced, copied, distributed, republished, modified, or commercially exploited without the prior permission of the copyright holder.

Contributors are solely responsible for ensuring that their submissions do not violate the copyright, intellectual property, or legal rights of any individual, organisation, or third party.

If you believe that any content published on Giga Bulletin infringes upon your copyright or intellectual property rights, please contact us with relevant details for review.

Adult Content Notice

Giga Bulletin may publish literary and artistic works intended for mature audiences. Certain poems, essays, stories, artworks, and cultural discussions may contain themes related to adulthood, human relationships, social realities, emotional experiences, or other mature subject matter.

By accessing this website, visitors acknowledge that some content may be intended for adult readers and audiences capable of engaging with mature literary and artistic expression.

Data Protection

Information shared with Giga Bulletin is handled with reasonable care and used only for editorial, communication, and publication purposes. While every effort is made to protect submitted information, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
